Sau-Chin Chen

Associate Professor of Cognitive Psychology

Tzu-Chi University

Sau-Chin Chen is the associate professor of Department of Human Development and Psychology, Tzu-Chi University since 2017. Between 2016 Feb and 2017 Jan, I was visiting Department of Psychology, Erasmus University Rotterdam. During this period, I collaborated with Rolf Zwaan on mental simulation in language comprehension.

Since 2017, I joined Psychological Science Accelerator and contributed to the “object orientation effects across languages” collaborative research project. To practice what I learned from The Society for the Improvement of Psychological Science, I am managing the podcast “open cafe” and running the community “Taiwan Collaboration for Psychological Scientific Research”. Both projects aim to ground open and reproducible sciences in Taiwan and Chinese-speaking societies.

Till 2021, I am participating in the operations of Psychological Science Accelerator and The Society for the Improvement of Psychological Science as the associative director and executive committee.

This website is updated based on my knowledge about data science. Since 2017, I have participated in COS Ambassador project and registered membership with The Society for the Improvement of Psychological Science (SIPS). This experience turned me toward interests in evaluating and improving psycholinguistic research quality. I am developing Chinese tutorials on open sciences and registered reports, and welcome sharing materials from my Registered Report Workshop.
